❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are all games really free?
Yes! Every game on GridZone is 100% free forever. No hidden fees, no premium versions, no paywalls.
Do I need to create an account?
Never. Just open the site and play — no sign-up, no email, no password required.
Can I suggest a new game?
Absolutely! We love suggestions. Reach out via Ko-fi or Reddit and tell us what game you'd like to see next.
I found a bug — how do I report it?
Please let us know via Ko-fi or Reddit with the game name and what happened. We fix bugs as fast as possible!
How can I support GridZone?
Share the site with friends, leave feedback, and if you want to go the extra mile — buy us a coffee on Ko-fi! Every bit helps keep the site running and growing.
Will there be more games?
Yes! We are constantly working on new games. Follow us on Reddit or check back regularly for new additions.

🐛 Report a bug

Something not working right? Tell us:

1. Which game has the issue

2. What device and browser you're using

3. What happened and what you expected to happen

Send this info to us via Ko-fi and we'll get it fixed as soon as possible. Thank you for helping make GridZone better!
