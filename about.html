🚀 Our Mission

GridZone was built with one simple goal — to give everyone access to fun, classic games for free. No downloads, no sign-ups, no paywalls. Just open your browser and play, on any device, anywhere in the world.

We believe great games should be accessible to everyone, whether you're on a phone in Karachi or a laptop in New York.

🎯 What is GridZone?

GridZone is a free online gaming site featuring classic browser-based games built from scratch with love. Every game on GridZone is hand-coded with smooth animations, sound effects, and a unique visual style that makes them a joy to play.

We started with one game and grew to a full collection — and we're adding more all the time!

👩‍💻 Who built this?

GridZone is an independent project built and maintained by a solo developer who is passionate about games and the open web. Every line of code, every animation, every sound effect was crafted with care to give you the best possible experience.

We're constantly working on new games and improvements. Have a suggestion? We'd love to hear from you!

💜 Why it's free

We genuinely believe games should be free and accessible to everyone. GridZone runs on passion and community support. If you enjoy what we've built, consider buying us a coffee — it helps keep the servers running and motivates us to build more!
