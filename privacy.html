📋 The Short Version

GridZone is a free casual gaming site. We don't collect your name, email, or any personal information. We may show ads (via Google AdSense) and use basic analytics to understand how people use the site. That's it.

1. Who We Are

GridZone ("we", "us", "our") is a free online gaming website available at tic-tac-toe-alpha-fawn-95.vercel.app. We offer free browser-based games with no registration required.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. We do not require you to create an account to use our games.

However, the following data may be collected automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Pages visited and time spent on site

  • General location (country/city level only, not precise)

This data is collected through third-party services (Google Analytics and Google AdSense) and is used only to improve the site and serve relevant ads.

3. Cookies

GridZone uses cookies — small text files stored on your device. These are used by:

  • Google AdSense — to show relevant advertisements
  • Google Analytics — to understand how visitors use our site

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Note that doing so may affect how some parts of the site function.

4. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites such as Ko-fi. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

GridZone is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is minimal risk associated with using GridZone. All games run entirely in your browser and no game data is sent to our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us via our Ko-fi page. We'll do our best to respond promptly.
